> [!INFO] Lesson Overview
> Successful investing is not about finding the perfect stock. It is about building a balanced portfolio. Asset Allocation and Diversification are two of the most important principles used by professional investors to reduce risk and achieve consistent long-term returns.

---

- Understand why diversification reduces risk.

- Avoid common portfolio management mistakes.

# What is Asset Allocation?

**Asset Allocation** is the process of distributing investments among different asset classes based on an investor's:

- Financial Goals
- Risk Tolerance
- Investment Horizon

Instead of investing all money in one asset, investors spread investments across multiple assets.

---

# Major Asset Classes

A diversified portfolio may include:

- Stocks (Equity)
- Mutual Funds
- Bonds
- Gold
- Fixed Deposits
- REITs
- Cash

Each asset behaves differently during different market conditions.

---

Investment Amount

₹10,00,000

| Asset | Allocation | Amount |
|--------|-----------:|-------:|
| Stocks | 40% | ₹4,00,000 |

| Bonds | 15% | ₹1,50,000 |
| Gold | 10% | ₹1,00,000 |
| Fixed Deposit | 10% | ₹1,00,000 |

This is only an example. The right allocation depends on each investor's personal circumstances.

---

# What is Diversification?

**Diversification** means spreading investments across different assets, industries, sectors, or companies to reduce overall investment risk.

Instead of relying on one investment, investors build a portfolio of multiple investments.

---

# Why Diversification Works

Suppose you invest everything in one company.

If that company performs poorly, your entire investment is affected.

If you invest across multiple companies and asset classes, poor performance in one investment may be offset by better performance in others.

# Types of Diversification

## 1. Company Diversification

Invest in multiple companies instead of one.

Example

- Company A
- Company B
- Company C

---

## 2. Sector Diversification

Invest across different industries.

Example

- Banking
- IT
- Automobile
- FMCG
- Healthcare
- Energy

---

## 3. Asset Diversification

Invest in different asset classes.

Example

- Stocks
- Bonds
- Gold
- Mutual Funds

---

## 4. Geographic Diversification

Invest in different countries or regions.

Example

- India
- United States
- International Funds

# Why Asset Allocation is Important

Asset Allocation helps investors:

- Reduce portfolio volatility.
- Improve long-term consistency.
- Manage market uncertainty.
- Protect capital during downturns.

Professional investors review and adjust their asset allocation periodically.

# Rebalancing

Over time, investments grow at different rates.

This changes the original asset allocation.

**Portfolio Rebalancing** is the process of restoring the intended allocation.

Example

Original

Stocks = 40%

After one year

Stocks = 52%

Rebalancing brings the allocation back toward the desired percentage.

---

# Important Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Asset Allocation | Distribution of investments across asset classes |
| Diversification | Spreading investments to reduce risk |
| Portfolio | Collection of investments |
| Rebalancing | Adjusting a portfolio to maintain target allocation |
| Volatility | Degree of price fluctuations |

---

# Common Mistakes Beginners Make

❌ Investing all money in one stock.

❌ Buying companies from only one sector.

❌ Ignoring Gold or Fixed Income.

❌ Never reviewing the portfolio.

❌ Chasing trending stocks without diversification.

---

# Professional Investor Tip

> [!TIP]
> Diversification reduces **unsystematic risk** (company-specific risk), but it cannot eliminate **systematic risk** (overall market risk). A balanced portfolio is designed to manage risk—not remove it entirely.

# Investor Checklist

Before investing, ask yourself:

- Am I investing in multiple asset classes?
- Is my portfolio diversified across sectors?
- Does my allocation match my financial goals?
- Have I reviewed my portfolio recently?
- Am I taking more risk than necessary?

---

# Key Takeaways

- Asset Allocation determines how investments are distributed.
- Diversification helps reduce investment risk.
- A balanced portfolio performs more consistently over time.
- Rebalancing maintains the desired asset allocation.
- Successful investing focuses on risk management as well as returns.

---

# Summary

Asset Allocation and Diversification are the cornerstones of portfolio management. Rather than concentrating investments in a single asset or company, investors should build diversified portfolios that align with their financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment horizon. A well-diversified portfolio provides greater stability and improves the potential for long-term wealth creation.
