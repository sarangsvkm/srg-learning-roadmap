> Compounding is one of the most powerful concepts in investing. It enables investors to generate returns not only on their original investment but also on the returns earned in previous periods. Long-term investors rely on compounding to build substantial wealth over time.

# What is Compounding?

**Compounding** is the process of earning returns on both your original investment (principal) and the returns that have already been generated.

Unlike simple interest, compounding allows your money to grow exponentially over time.

---

# The Basic Principle

```
Money Invested
       │
       ▼
Earn Return
       │
       ▼
Reinvest the Return
       │
       ▼
Earn Return on the Previous Return
       │
       ▼
Wealth Grows Faster
```

---

# Simple Interest vs Compounding

## Simple Interest

Interest is earned only on the original investment.

### Example

Investment = ₹100,000

Interest = 10%

Every year

Interest = ₹10,000

After 5 Years

Total Value

₹150,000

---

## Compound Growth

Interest is earned on:

- Original Investment
- Previous Interest

Example

Investment = ₹100,000

Return = 10%

| Year | Investment Value |
|------|-----------------:|
| 1 | ₹110,000 |
| 2 | ₹121,000 |
| 3 | ₹133,100 |
| 4 | ₹146,410 |
| 5 | ₹161,051 |

Notice how the annual increase becomes larger every year.

---

# Compound Interest Formula

```
Future Value = P × (1 + r)^n
```

Where

- **P** = Principal Investment
- **r** = Annual Rate of Return
- **n** = Number of Years

---

# Why Time is Important

Time is the most powerful factor in compounding.

The longer your investment remains invested, the faster it grows.

Small investments made early often outperform large investments made later.

---

# Example

### Investor A

Starts at Age 25

Monthly Investment

₹5,000

Investment Period

30 Years

Annual Return

12%

Approximate Wealth

₹1.75 Crore+

---

### Investor B

Starts at Age 35

Monthly Investment

₹5,000

Investment Period

20 Years

Annual Return

12%

Approximate Wealth

₹50 Lakhs+

---

Although both invested the same monthly amount, Investor A accumulated significantly more wealth simply by starting earlier.

---

# Rule of 72

The **Rule of 72** estimates how long an investment takes to double.

Formula

```
72 ÷ Annual Return (%)
```

Example

Return = 12%

```
72 ÷ 12 = 6 Years
```

Your investment approximately doubles every **6 years**.

- Accelerates wealth creation.
- Rewards long-term investing.
- Increases investment returns over time.
- Encourages disciplined investing.
- Reduces dependence on frequent trading.

| Investment Duration | Longer duration produces exponential growth |
| Regular Contributions | Accelerates compounding |

# Common Mistakes Beginners Make

❌ Starting investments too late.

❌ Frequently withdrawing invested money.

❌ Stopping SIP during market corrections.

❌ Expecting quick profits.

❌ Ignoring the power of long-term investing.
